## Chronological Model Evaluation

- Created `src/temporal_baseline.py`.
- Evaluated a Dummy Regressor and a Random Forest using earlier dates for training and later dates for testing.
- Training rows: 60,800.
- Testing rows: 15,200.
- Dummy baseline: MAE 32.951, RMSE 41.622, R² -0.008.
- Random Forest: MAE 22.211, RMSE 29.375, R² 0.498.
- Saved comparison results to `outputs/temporal_baseline_results.csv`.

## Interpretation

The Random Forest outperformed the Dummy Regressor on all three reported metrics. Its performance was weaker than the earlier random-split evaluation, highlighting the importance of chronological validation.

## Limitations

These results measure predictive performance, not the causal effect of price or discounts. Further validation is required before interpreting any estimated pricing effect causally.
