# Day 4 — Product Price Variation Analysis

**Date:** 17 October 2026

## Objective
Investigate whether the same product at the same store has different prices across different dates.

## Tasks Performed
- Created `src/price_variation_analysis.py`.
- Grouped data by `Store ID` and `Product ID`.
- Counted unique prices for each product-store combination.
- Identified combinations with changing prices.

## Findings
- Total product-store combinations: 100
- Combinations with changing prices: 100

## Limitations
This analysis identifies price variation but does not establish why prices changed or whether those changes affected sales.
