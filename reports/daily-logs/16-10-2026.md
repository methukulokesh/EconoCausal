# Day 3 — Price and Sales Relationship Analysis

**Date:** 16 October 2026

## Objective

Analyze the relationship between product price and units sold.

## Tasks Performed

- Calculated the correlation between `Price` and `Units Sold`.
- Created a scatter plot to visualize the relationship.
- Saved the chart as `outputs/figures/price_vs_units_sold.png`.

## Findings

- Price versus units sold correlation: 0.0011.
- The dataset shows almost no linear relationship between price and units sold.

## Limitations

Correlation alone cannot establish whether price causes sales to change.

## Next Steps

- Investigate price changes for the same product at the same store across different dates.
- Identify suitable control variables before attempting causal modeling.
